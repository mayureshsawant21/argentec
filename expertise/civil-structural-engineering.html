Civil & Structural Engineering

The structure is the part of a building nobody sees and everybody depends on. We execute foundations and frames with engineering discipline, from the first pile to the last slab.

Discuss your project Capabilities & scope
02.A · OverviewCivil & Structural Engineering

Structures built to outlast their brief.

Argentec delivers civil and structural works for residential towers, commercial buildings and institutional projects. Our teams translate structural drawings into sound, accurately set-out structures, working closely with the project's structural consultant.

We use systems such as MIVAN aluminium formwork where they suit the design. These systems allow repetitive floor cycles, consistent concrete finishes and less timber waste, which matters on multi-storey projects in dense Indian cities.

  • Faster repeatable cycles

    Formwork systems chosen for speed and repetition on typical floors.

  • Accuracy you can build on

    Set-out, levels and alignment checked so finishes and services fit first time.

  • Less waste on site

    Reusable formwork and planned pours reduce material wastage and rework.

02.F · FAQsCivil & Structural Engineering

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide structural design?

Structural design is carried out by the project's appointed structural consultant. Argentec's role is engineering-led execution: reviewing, coordinating and building to that design.

When is MIVAN aluminium formwork the right choice?

It suits buildings with many repetitive floors, such as residential towers, where speed of cycle and finish quality matter. For irregular or low-rise structures, conventional formwork may be more economical. We advise case by case.

How do you control concrete quality?

Through approved mix designs, supplier checks, slump and cube testing, pour planning and curing supervision, all recorded against our quality checklist.

Can you take up only the civil and structural package?

Yes. Civil and structural works can be contracted as a package or as part of a wider turnkey or EPC scope.

Do you work on redevelopment sites in dense areas?

Yes. Dense urban sites need careful planning for access, neighbouring structures and material storage, which we address in the method statement.
