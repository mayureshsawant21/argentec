MEP & Building Services

A building only works when its services do. We coordinate and install the electrical, plumbing and fire systems that make spaces safe, comfortable and ready to occupy.

Discuss your project Capabilities & scope
03.A · OverviewMEP & Building Services

The systems that make a building work.

Argentec delivers mechanical, electrical and plumbing works with the structure in mind, not after it. Services are coordinated with the civil programme from the start, so sleeves, shafts and routes are ready when installation begins.

Our MEP scope covers electrical, plumbing, fire-fighting and high- and low-voltage systems, from detailed engineering coordination to installation, testing and handover.

  • Coordinated, not retro-fitted

    Service routes planned with the structure to avoid clashes and core-cutting.

  • One accountable team

    Civil and MEP under one contractor reduces interface gaps.

  • Ready to occupy

    Testing and commissioning planned into the programme, not left to the end.

03.F · FAQsMEP & Building Services

Frequently asked questions

Which MEP services does Argentec cover?

Electrical, plumbing, fire-fighting and high- and low-voltage systems, from detailed engineering coordination to execution. Other specialist systems can be coordinated through approved specialist contractors.

Do you design MEP systems?

MEP design is normally provided by the project's MEP consultant. We review, coordinate and develop shop drawings for installation.

Can MEP be awarded separately from civil works?

Yes. It can be a standalone package or part of a combined civil and MEP or turnkey scope.

How do you avoid clashes between services and structure?

Combined services drawings are reconciled with the structural and architectural drawings before work starts, and openings are confirmed before each pour.

What do we receive at handover?

Test reports, commissioning records, as-built drawings and operation and maintenance information for each system.
