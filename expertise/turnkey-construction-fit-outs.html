Turnkey Construction & Fit-Outs

Hand us the brief and receive the keys. Turnkey delivery takes a project through the complete cycle, from engineering to a finished, ready-to-use space.

Discuss your project Capabilities & scope
06.A · OverviewTurnkey Construction & Fit-Outs

From brief to keys in hand.

Argentec undertakes turnkey projects, large and small, taking them through the complete cycle from engineering to completion. The client works with one team and receives a finished, ready-to-use building or space.

For occupiers and owners, we also deliver interior fit-outs: converting bare shell space into finished offices, retail and institutional interiors, coordinated with building services and the landlord's requirements.

  • One brief, one team

    A single contractor responsible for every trade and finish.

  • Predictable handover

    Programme built backwards from the date you need to move in.

  • Finish quality controlled

    Mock-ups, samples and snagging managed by the same team that builds.

06.F · FAQsTurnkey Construction & Fit-Outs

Frequently asked questions

What does turnkey include?

Everything needed to deliver a finished, usable building or space to the agreed brief: coordination of design, procurement, construction, services, finishes and handover.

Do you do interior fit-outs only, without the building?

Interior fit-out for offices, retail and institutional spaces is part of this service. Please share your brief and we will confirm scope.

Can you work in an occupied building?

Yes, with agreed working hours, noise and dust control, and coordination with building management or the housing society.

Who designs the interiors?

You can bring your own interior designer, or we can coordinate a design consultant as part of a design-and-build turnkey scope.

How is the price agreed?

Turnkey work is priced on a defined scope and specification, with any client changes handled through agreed variations.
