For housing societies

Redevelopment, step by step.

Redevelopment is one of the biggest decisions a society makes. This is how the process typically runs; the details depend on your building, your members and the approvals involved.

  1. 1

    Resolution & feasibility

    The society agrees to explore redevelopment and studies what the plot, the rules and its members' needs allow.

  2. 2

    Choosing a developer

    Proposals are compared on area, terms, track record and timeline, then the society selects a partner.

  3. 3

    Agreement

    The development agreement sets out each member's new home, temporary accommodation and the schedule.

  4. 4

    Approvals

    Plans are submitted to the planning authority, and construction starts once the permissions are in place.

  5. 5

    Build & handover

    Members move out, the new building is constructed, and they return to new homes on completion.
